steps

Step 1: Create a square piece of foam.

Step 2: Fold in half to make a triangle, do the same on the other side.

Step 3: Make a hole where the creases meet.

Step 4: Create a square piece of foam.
Make cuts half way though the square from each corner to the center.

Step 5: Bring all outer holes together so you can put a pipecleaner through.

Step 6: using a circular tool, create a impression in the foam. Cut that out and make a hole in the middle. make 2

Step 7: wrap the pipecleaner around the straw tightly.

Step 8: put the circular pieces though the pipecleaner as well as your windmill finish with another circle.

Step 9: cut the excess off and wrap it tight

Step 10: enjoy your windmill fan!
